## 3. Revenue split: e-commerce service fee vs interest/treasury vs other; e-auction GMV and commission rate

  Rs 349 cr in FY23 (+1% YoY). Source: CARE Ratings press release on MSTC Limited (dated on the PR

- Total Operating Income (TOI) FY24 is reported by CARE as DOWN ~3% YoY, attributed to a decline in net
  revenue from the trading segment - i.e. even though e-commerce/service income grew, overall TOI fell
  because the (now largely wound-down) trading segment shrank further. This is consistent with the task
  brief's description of a legacy trading business being run down.
- E-auction transaction value (GMV): NOT FOUND as a reported MSTC figure for any year in this session.
  One indirect data point was found: an independent analyst newsletter (gauravtambade.substack.com,
  content not directly fetchable) is summarized as estimating MSTC's "net commission retention" at 2.8%,
  described as derived from a 4% gross transaction fee prescribed under CPCB's EPR (Extended Producer
  Responsibility) trading guidelines, net of a regulatory remittance to CPCB, and stating this "as per
  FY25 volumes translates to approximately Rs 971 crore." IMPORTANT CAVEAT: this 2.8%/4% figure appears
  to relate specifically to the CPCB EPR-certificate trading vertical (one sub-business), NOT MSTC's
  overall blended e-auction commission rate across its core verticals (metal scrap, coal, iron ore,
  forest produce, general merchandise/surplus, NPA/asset sales, real estate). It is an analyst estimate,
  not a company-reported figure, and the Rs 971 cr itself is ambiguous as to whether it denotes GMV or
  commission income for that vertical (given MSTC's total FY25 revenue-from-operations was only ~Rs 311
  cr, Rs 971 cr cannot be total company-wide commission income, so it likely denotes GMV for the EPR
  vertical alone, or is simply unreliable). DO NOT use this as an overall-GMV or overall-commission-rate
  figure in the final report without independent verification.

## 4. Balance sheet FY22-FY26

All figures are as scraped by data aggregators via WebSearch summaries; exact "as of" dates are
themselves sometimes inconsistent in the source text (flagged inline).

- Net worth: Rs 903.94 cr, stated by CARE as "+22.42% YoY" (period-end date not clearly stated in the
  search snippet, inferred FY24 context from the surrounding CARE commentary). A separate source states
  stockholders' equity of Rs 8.89B (Rs 889 cr) labelled inconsistently as "FY25 (as of March 2024)" -
  broadly consistent in magnitude with the CARE figure, reinforcing confidence in a ~Rs 850-900 cr net
  worth figure around the FY24 year-end, but exact date not nailed down.
- A THIRD, materially different, figure: total equity of Rs 7.38B (Rs 738 cr) "as of December 2025"
  (Q3 FY26). If accurate this would represent a decline in net worth versus the ~Rs 900 cr FY24 figure
  despite two years of positive PAT and dividends paid in between - internally implausible unless (a) the
  dividend payout (including the ~Rs 32/share special dividend potentially tied to FSNL proceeds) exceeded
  cumulative retained profit over FY25-FY26, which is plausible given the scale of dividends discussed
  above, or (b) this Rs 738 cr figure is on a different consolidation basis or simply another data-quality
  issue. FLAG AS UNRESOLVED - needs the actual FY26 balance sheet.
- Cash + short-term investments: Rs 11.48B (Rs 1,148 cr) as of "March 2024" per one source; Rs 13.6B
  (Rs 1,360 cr) as of the "most recent period" (undated, likely ~Dec-2025) per another. Directionally
  consistent with a cash-rich, growing treasury book.
- Total assets: Rs 22.49B (Rs 2,249 cr) around FY24/25; Rs 21.18B (Rs 2,118 cr) as of Dec-2025 (Q3 FY26) -
  a modest decline, plausibly explained by the large FY25/FY26 dividend outflows (including the special
  dividend) exceeding retained earnings and any EMD-float swings.
- Total liabilities: Rs 13.61B (Rs 1,361 cr) around FY24/25; Rs 13.80B (Rs 1,380 cr) as of Dec-2025.
- Total debt / borrowings: Rs 1.45B (Rs 145 cr), giving a reported debt/equity of ~19.6% in one source.
  This is broadly consistent with CARE's own gearing figures of 0.22x (FY24) and 0.24x (FY23) - i.e.
  MSTC does carry SOME reported borrowings (not literally zero), but at a low, PSU-typical level, likely
  working-capital / bank-guarantee-linked rather than term debt. The task brief's expectation of
  "near-zero borrowings" is directionally confirmed but "near-zero" is not literally "zero."
- Trade receivables: Rs 407 cr as of 31-Mar-2022 declining to Rs 333 cr, per one source (exact date of
  the Rs 333 cr figure not clear from the search snippet - could be FY23 or FY24). A separate, seemingly
  older reference (likely from an investor call circa FY20-21, undated precisely) cites "standalone
  balance sheet receivables of around 723 crores" of which marketing/e-commerce receivables were said to
  be "only 50 crores... considered risky" with the rest backed by bank guarantees (~110% cover). These
  two receivables figures (Rs 333-407 cr vs Rs 723 cr) are NOT on a consistent timeline and should not be
  averaged or treated as a trend without the actual annual report Trade Receivables note for each year.
- EMD / security deposits held on behalf of auction participants (a liability item per the task brief):
  NOT FOUND as a balance sheet line figure in any year. Multiple searches returned only generic
  descriptions of how EMD works operationally (non-interest-bearing deposits, refund mechanics, MSTC's
  "Global EMD Management System"), never the actual balance-sheet quantum. This is a MAJOR GAP flagged
  explicitly by the task brief as "a big liability item" - needs the annual report Balance Sheet /

  session.
- Trade payables: NOT FOUND as a distinct figure.
- Contingent liabilities: ~Rs 1,517 cr as on 31-Mar-2025 per an ICRA rating rationale (dated 15-Jan-2026

This is a genuinely messy area in the source data - multiple mutually-contradictory per-share and

- 2020 (dated only as "2020", fiscal year mapping unconfirmed): annual dividend Rs 3.30/share (one source).

  FY24-25), consistent with a regular interim dividend cadence.
- A special/large dividend of Rs 32.00/share is cited, associated in the search text with "February 2025"

  filing (bseindia.com blocked). HIGH-PRIORITY item for the "use of FSNL proceeds" question in notes-03.

  11-Feb-2026, record date 18-Feb-2026) - this figure is corroborated across multiple independent search

- Payout ratio: NOT COMPUTED - would require a clean, verified full-year PAT and total-dividend-paid

dividend note, not from this session's search-engine summaries.
