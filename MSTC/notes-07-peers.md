## 3.1 Direct competitors - private/unlisted e-procurement and e-auction companies

MSTC's closest functional competitors in *pure e-procurement/e-auction software and services* are private, much smaller companies. None are listed on NSE/BSE as far as this research could establish (flagging one exception-candidate below where listing status is ambiguous).

**C1 India Pvt Ltd** (New Delhi HQ) - e-procurement/e-auction SaaS provider since 2000, one of the first in India on a SaaS delivery model.
- Revenue: conflicting figures found - Getlatka (a private-company data aggregator) cites **$31.4 million ARR** for 2025; a separate filings-based citation gives **Rs 20.3 crore for FY ended 31-Mar-2024**. These are roughly 15-16x apart even after FX conversion (~$31.4M ≈ Rs 260 crore vs Rs 20.3 crore) - **flag as an unreconciled discrepancy**, plausibly because one figure is global/all-entity ARR (including non-Indian subsidiaries or bookings, not just Indian statutory revenue) and the other is the standalone Indian entity's audited FY24 revenue. Treat the Rs 20.3 crore figure as more reliable for India-specific comparison to MSTC.
- Bootstrapped (no external funding); ~285 employees; disclosed private valuation ~$94.1 million (Crunchbase-sourced, unverified).
- Clients cited: State Bank of India, Union Bank, Aircel, Hero MotoCorp, HP, BHEL; early public-sector win was Andhra Pradesh government's e-marketplace in 2002 (won a Golden Icon e-governance award).
- **Scale conclusion: even at the higher revenue estimate, C1 India is roughly 1/15th to 1/20th of MSTC's revenue scale** - not a scale peer, but a genuine functional/technology competitor for e-procurement software mandates specifically (as distinct from MSTC's fee-based auction-execution model).

**eProcurement Technologies Ltd** (brand ABC Procure / abcprocure.com; also operates TenderTiger.com, described as "India's largest tender portal"), Ahmedabad-based, ~20 years old, ISO/IEC 27001 certified end-to-end eProcurement provider (eTendering, eAuction, reverse auction, eRFQ, spend analysis, category management, SRM).
- Revenue: **Rs 10-50 crore band for FY ended 31-Mar-2025** (thecompanycheck.com, a registrar-filings aggregator - wide band, not a precise figure). Authorised share capital Rs 10 crore, paid-up capital Rs 5.75 crore.
- **NSE/BSE listing status: NOT CONFIRMED.** The task brief's framing implied this might be a listed comparable; no market cap, share price, or exchange trading data was found in this research pass, suggesting it is likely either unlisted or too thinly traded/obscure to appear in standard financial-data aggregator searches. Flag for direct verification (check NSE/BSE company search directly) before treating as a listed peer.
- Scale: at most Rs 50 crore revenue vs MSTC's ~Rs 370-450 crore (FY26) - roughly 1/10th or smaller.

**Antares Systems Ltd** (brand TenderWizard), Bangalore-based, founded 1997 as a software product company; offices across major Indian cities.
- Claims **500,000 tenders processed electronically and Rs 4,00,000 crore (Rs 4 lakh crore) cumulative procurement value transacted on the platform** (company-sourced claim via theenterpriseworld.com - this is a *cumulative, lifetime* figure, not annual GMV, and not independently audited/verified; do not compare directly to MSTC's or GeM's annual GMV figures without adjusting for the multi-year accumulation period).
- First Indian e-procurement system STQC-certified for full CVC-guideline compliance (a genuine differentiator/credibility marker for winning government contracts).
- No listed-company financial data found - appears to be a private company. Revenue/profitability NOT FOUND.

**NexTenders** - NOT FOUND as a distinct identifiable entity in this research pass; likely either a smaller regional player, a product/brand name under another company, or a name variant not indexed under that exact spelling by the sources searched. Flag for direct follow-up (try alternate spellings or ask user for source).

**TCIL (Telecommunications Consultants India Ltd) and NICSI (National Informatics Centre Services Inc.)** - both are Government of India entities in the broader IT/e-governance services space, but neither was established in this research pass as a primary e-auction/e-procurement competitor to MSTC specifically (TCIL is telecom-consulting focused; NICSI is NIC's implementation arm, more aligned with CPPP-style tendering infrastructure than disposal auctions). Treat as tangential, not core comps, pending further research. NOT deeply researched given time/search-budget constraints this session.

**ITI Ltd** - this is a PSU **telecom equipment manufacturer** (see 3.2 below for its valuation as a PSU comparable), not an e-procurement/e-auction competitor. It appears in the task brief's peer list as a general PSU valuation comparable, not a direct competitor - retained only in the listed-PSU-peer table below, not in this competitor section.

**Metal-scrap-specific private auction/trading platforms** - the task brief flagged these as worth researching; this pass did not surface a specific, named, at-scale private Indian metal-scrap e-auction platform competing directly with MSTC's scrap-auction business (as distinct from physical scrap traders/recyclers like Gravita India, EMS, Namo eWaste, which are recyclers/processors, not auction-platform operators, and therefore not true platform competitors to MSTC even though they are counterparties/bidders on MSTC's platform). If the higher end of the range is accurate, MSTC's capital efficiency would actually be competitive with CAMS (53.6%) and MCX (65.67%); if the lower end is accurate, it lags most of the exchange cluster meaningfully.

## 3.3 Peers in distress, exiting, or under strategic review

- **MMTC** pays no dividend despite recurring profits and trades at 4.26x book value - worth flagging as a capital-allocation outlier among the PSU trading peers, though not "distressed" in an operating sense.
- **IRCON** posted an 18.14% profit decline in FY26 - a genuine, quantified earnings setback among the PSU peer set, contemporaneous with MSTC's own (larger) decline.
- No peer in this table was found to be actively "exiting" its business or undergoing insolvency/distress in this research pass.
- **MSTC itself is reported to be planning a strategic exit from its own trading vertical in FY27**, per financial-media coverage of company commentary ("MSTC Ltd Posts Revenue Growth; Plans Trading Vertical Exit" - Whalesbook), to refocus on digital/e-commerce services. This is company-specific strategic news, not a peer-distress signal, but is directly relevant to interpreting MSTC's own forward revenue mix and should be cross-checked against the FY26 annual report/investor presentation for exact scope and timing.

## 3.4 Stated milestones peers are pursuing

- **MCX:** delivering very strong recent growth (Q1 FY27 total income +85% YoY, PAT +103% YoY) - momentum/expansion phase, likely driving its rich multiple.
- **CERO (MSTC's own JV, not a peer but relevant context):** management (Sumit Issar) has stated an aim to reach capacity to dismantle **1 million end-of-life vehicles by FY 2026-27** - label explicitly as a **management target/aspiration, not an achieved figure.**
- **MSTC itself:** per financial-media coverage of recent earnings commentary, is (a) planning to exit its trading vertical in FY27, (b) building a **CPCB EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility) certificate trading exchange**, expected to start generating revenue from FY27 with "good double-digit growth projected once the exchange stabilises" (management commentary, 9M FY26 earnings call, via secondary coverage - label as forward-looking/unaudited), and (c) reportedly entering a **new travel-business vertical** (Whalesbook headline, "MSTC Ltd Reports FY26 Profit Drop; Proposes Rs 8.10 Dividend, Plans Travel Business Entry") - this diversification move is notable and somewhat unusual for a PSU e-auction platform; **flag for scrutiny in the strategy/governance sections of the broader research** as a potential capital-allocation or focus concern given it is unrelated to MSTC's core e-auction competency, alongside the fact it comes in the same period as a sharp profit decline.
- **IEX:** no specific FY27 milestone found in this pass beyond its current discount-to-peer-multiple positioning; NOT FOUND.
- Other listed PSU peers (RITES, IRCON, EIL): no specific forward milestones surfaced in this research pass beyond ordinary order-book/execution commentary; NOT FOUND at the depth requested - flag for follow-up if a deeper peer-strategy comparison is needed.
