## 18.0 Does MSTC report a conventional order book? - VERIFIED: NO, confirmed by absence, not by an explicit disclaimer

Targeted search for "order book" / "order inflow" language in MSTC's annual report, presentations, and transcripts returned no snippet using that terminology for MSTC. Every disclosed operating metric found in this research is instead one of:
- Rupee value of goods/assets transacted through the e-commerce portal in a period ("Gross Merchandise Value" / "value of goods transacted through the portal") - a flow metric over a quarter or year, not a backlog/pipeline stock metric.
- Individual named work orders/contracts won (e.g. the Coal India e-auction work order, the Coal India NRS linkage mandate) with a stated multi-year term but NOT rolled up into a disclosed aggregate "order book" figure in rupee terms.
- Event counts for specific mandates (e.g. "166 auction events" under the CIL 2-year contract).

This is consistent with MSTC's business model: it earns a percentage commission on transactions it facilitates as they occur, not on a contracted future revenue stream it can point to as backlog. A manufacturing-style "order book" concept does not map cleanly onto a fee-on-GMV service business, and MSTC (on the evidence found) does not attempt to construct one for investor communication. CAVEAT: this is an absence-of-evidence finding from search snippets, not a direct read of the annual report's MD&A section confirming MSTC explicitly states it does not report an order book - that explicit-disclaimer check needs a primary-source read.

6. **Indian Railways scrap e-auction - LOST MANDATE, confirmed and dated to origin**
   HISTORY: A pilot online-auction-for-scrap-sales project began in 2006-07 on Southern Railway using software supplied by MSTC (then "Metal Scrap Trading Corporation"). Railways subsequently decided to build its e-auction software IN-HOUSE after MSTC sought charges of approximately 1.5-2% of total sale proceeds - i.e. Railways balked at MSTC's proposed take rate and built its own capability rather than pay it.
   CURRENT STATE: Indian Railways now runs its own e-auction system for scrap sales through IREPS (Indian Railways E-Procurement System, ireps.gov.in), which is explicitly described in a current source as "the official website of Indian Railways for E-Auction of Railway scrap Materials." This is a live, currently-operating alternative to MSTC, not a historical footnote that was later reversed.
   ASSESSMENT: this is the clearest, best-documented BEAR-CASE PRECEDENT available in this research - a major, high-volume PSU counterparty (Indian Railways is one of India's largest scrap generators) walked away from MSTC specifically over the take-rate MSTC proposed, and built durable in-house replacement infrastructure rather than return. It directly demonstrates the "government builds its own portal instead of paying MSTC's commission" risk named in the business-model UNKNOWN UNKNOWNS section is not hypothetical.
   CAVEAT: the exact date of the 2006-07-era decision to go in-house was not pinned down beyond "the Railways decided" (no single dated announcement found); this is a multi-year-old, long-settled situation, not new information, but it appears NOT to be commonly surfaced in current MSTC bull-case coverage, which is itself notable.
   Source: search-synthesized summary citing the 2006-07 Southern Railway pilot and the subsequent in-house build decision; IREPS current-operation confirmed via ireps.gov.in listing itself (live site, "E-Auctions Indian Railways Sale & Scrap through e-Auction").

9. **IBA / PSB Alliance - bank-asset e-auction platform (eBKray / BAANKNET) - MANDATE STATUS HAS MATERIALLY DEGRADED, best bear-case find of this research**
   ORIGIN: the Indian Banks' Association (IBA) engaged MSTC to develop an online common e-auction platform for member-bank asset sales. Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman launched this platform, "eBkray" (also written "eBKray" / "e-BKray"), on 28-Dec-2019, integrated with the IBAPI (Indian Banks Auctions Mortgaged Properties Information) portal. MSTC's own e-commerce site still hosts a "Bank Property E-auction" page under an ibapi path (mstcecommerce.com/auctionhome/ibapi/index.jsp), indicating MSTC retained at least a technical/hosting footprint historically.
   MIGRATION: current-period sources (2024-2025) describe eBKray as "owned and managed by PSB Alliance Private Limited" (a company jointly owned by the 12 public-sector banks) and "presently being managed by Indian Bank." A revamped e-auction portal branded "BAANKNET" (Bank Asset Auction Network) was launched 03-Jan-2025 under PSB Alliance ownership, per a PIB press release title ("Government Launches BAANKNET and e-BKray to enhance PSU Bank E-Auctions for Asset Sales") and multiple current-affairs/legal-update sites (insightsonindia.com, currentaffairs.adda247.com, fintechbiznews.com, a2ztaxcorp.net - 25-Mar-2025-dated coverage cluster). Separately, effective 01-Apr-2025, the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) mandated EXCLUSIVE use of the eBKray platform for asset sales under the liquidation process (IBBI Circular No. IBBI/LIQ/78/2024, dated 29-Oct-2024, in force from 01-Nov-2024, with the exclusivity mandate effective 01-Apr-2025) - this materially expands eBKray's mandated usage, but the platform by this point is described as PSB-Alliance/Indian-Bank-managed, not MSTC-operated.
   ASSESSMENT: MSTC appears to have built the original platform and IBAPI integration but the OPERATING AND OWNERSHIP ROLE has migrated to PSB Alliance / Indian Bank, with the newest branded surface (BAANKNET) explicitly a PSB Alliance product. This matches the task brief's flagged concern ("there were reports of it moving to PSB Alliance") almost exactly, and this research corroborates it across multiple independent, dated sources rather than a single rumor. NOT FOUND: whether MSTC retains any residual technology-licensing fee, back-end hosting role, or transition-period revenue from this platform, or whether this was a clean, complete hand-off with zero ongoing MSTC involvement - this distinction matters financially and needs a direct MSTC disclosure or IBA/PSB Alliance statement to resolve.
   Sources: PIB (pib.gov.in press release, title captured via search, could not fetch full text - egress blocked), insightsonindia.com (25-Mar-2025), currentaffairs.adda247.com, fintechbiznews.com, a2ztaxcorp.net, psballiance.com (own site, "BAANKNET (Bank Asset Auction Network)" page), ibclaw.in (IBBI circular summary, 29-Oct-2024 circular / 01-Nov-2024 and 01-Apr-2025 effective dates), business-standard.com (30-Oct-2024, "IBBI, IBA to launch centralised platform for asset liquidation auctions").

## 18.5 Growth strategy as management states it - numbered, with capability judgement

Management's growth-strategy statements were NOT independently retrievable as a clean numbered list from any earnings-call transcript or investor-presentation full text this session (all such documents sat behind blocked domains). The following is reconstructed from scattered result-commentary snippets and should be treated as a partial, indirect reading of strategy, not a verbatim management list:

1. Win/retain large recurring government e-auction mandates (Coal India being the clearest recent example, with TWO separate mandates won within an 11-month window - Mar-2025 and Feb-2026). JUDGEMENT: this directly extends MSTC's demonstrated core capability (running large-scale competitive e-auctions for a sophisticated PSU counterparty) and is the most credible growth strand found.
2. Expand vehicle-scrapping/recycling capacity via the CERO JV toward the stated 1-million-ELV-by-FY27 ambition. JUDGEMENT: partially extends demonstrated capability (MSTC's auction/registration/compliance know-how is relevant to a regulated recycling business) but also requires genuinely new operational capability (physical dismantling, environmental compliance, scrap-metal processing) that is NOT part of MSTC's historical core competence - execution risk here is real and unquantified in this research.
3. Land and state-asset monetisation auctions (Telangana precedent). JUDGEMENT: extends demonstrated capability directly - this is the same auction-platform skill applied to a new asset class (land instead of scrap/coal/minerals), a natural adjacency.
4. Entry into a "travel business" (per the single Whalesbook headline reference around FY26 results). JUDGEMENT: this does NOT obviously extend any demonstrated MSTC capability (travel is a consumer-facing, margin-different, logistics-heavy business unlike anything in MSTC's e-auction/e-procurement history) - flag as the least-grounded growth strand found, and NOT FOUND whether it has moved beyond a stated plan.
5. Formal exit of the legacy Trading segment (Q1 FY27), freeing management/capital attention from a low-return legacy line. JUDGEMENT: this is a subtraction/cleanup strategy, not a growth strand per se, but is relevant context - it signals management is at least willing to close down underperforming legacy lines rather than let them linger indefinitely.
