The theme the market is paying for is not the theme MSTC is exposed to

MSTC rose about 57 per cent in the month after the Delhi-NCR scrappage approval, on roughly thirteen times normal volume, and gained a further 7 per cent on 29-Jun-2026 on scrappage incentives in a Delhi electric-vehicle policy. That is the vehicle-scrapping theme being priced into the parent.

But MSTC's scrapping exposure sits in a 50:50 joint venture that is equity accounted, not consolidated, and whose revenue, profit and vehicle throughput have never been published. Meanwhile the business that actually produces MSTC's Rs 225 cr of operating profit, government auctions, has nothing to do with Delhi-NCR trucks.

On the thematic classification asked of this section: the vehicle-scrappage theme is currently crowded and being paid for; the government-auction franchise is neither crowded nor being paid for. An investor is buying the second and being charged for the first.

The mandate wins nobody is pricing

Between Sep-2025 and Mar-2026 MSTC picked up a pollution control board approval for an electronic trading platform, a Karnataka excise department mandate for liquor licence auctions, a nomination for the gold tariff rate quota auction, and a three-year Coal India non-regulated-sector linkage mandate. None is individually large. Together they show a company still winning new categories, which is the strongest available counter to the mandate-erosion bear case in Section 15, and the stock did not re-rate on any of them.

Institutional ownership is near-nil, and that cuts both ways

Mutual funds hold roughly 0.10 per cent. Every institutional category combined is roughly 5 to 6 per cent. Roughly 29 to 30 per cent of the whole company, which is about 85 per cent of the free float, is retail.

Bearish read: there is no institutional anchor, no analyst discipline, and a register that behaves like momentum capital. The 57 per cent move in Jun-2026 on a policy headline, and the 17 per cent single-day move on a small fund's 1.17 per cent purchase in Oct-2024, are both signatures of a shallow float rather than of re-appraised fundamentals.

Bullish read: if the operating story holds through the Mar-2027 renewal, there is no institutional position to be distributed and any institutional interest at all is incremental demand into a float worth about Rs 1,800 cr.
