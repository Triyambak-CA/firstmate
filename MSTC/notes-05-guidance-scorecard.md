## 1. Does MSTC host earnings concalls? YES - and this itself is a finding worth stating plainly

MSTC listed on 29-Mar-2019. Contrary to a plausible prior that a PSU might not hold analyst calls
at all, or might have started very recently, **MSTC does hold regular investor/analyst conference
calls, hosted by Equirus Securities**, and appears to have done so roughly quarterly for close to
four years by the report date.

**Earliest confirmed call found: on or around November 2022** (one snippet: "a conference call
with investors and analysts was held by MSTC Limited in November 2022, with a transcript...
provided for information and records" - this would correspond to Q2 FY23, quarter ended
30-Sep-2022). **No call earlier than November 2022 was found** despite a targeted search for
"first earnings call 2022" and similar queries. If this is genuinely the first call, **MSTC waited
roughly 3.5 years post-listing (Mar-2019 to Nov-2022) before beginning to hold analyst calls at
all** - itself a governance/IR-quality finding: a listed company (even a PSU) leaving analysts and
retail shareholders without a structured management interaction channel for three-plus years after
an IPO. This should be flagged in any credibility assessment: MSTC's guidance track record is
effectively **only ~4 years deep**, not the ~7.5 years since listing.
